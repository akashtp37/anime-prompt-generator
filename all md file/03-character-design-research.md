# Character Design Research

## Character Design System

The character generator should not treat every attribute as an unrelated random word. The research identifies several visual-design systems.

## Shape Language

### Circle / curved forms
Useful for:
- Warmth
- Approachability
- Innocence
- Flexibility
- Youthfulness

### Square / rectangular forms
Useful for:
- Strength
- Stability
- Dependability
- Authority
- Guardians
- Mentors

### Triangle / sharp forms
Useful for:
- Speed
- Tension
- Danger
- Aggression
- Unpredictability
- Assassins
- Rogues

## Silhouette Readability

Characters should remain identifiable from their major silhouette before small surface details are considered.

### Primary shapes
- Body stance
- Head shape
- Hair silhouette
- Main costume silhouette
- Primary weapon/gear

### Secondary shapes
- Clothing folds
- Armor sections
- Large straps
- Limb overlaps
- Major costume divisions

### Tertiary details
- Eyes
- Seams
- Buckles
- Small accessories
- Fine decorative details

## Color-Role Relationships

| Palette | Example colors | Character roles |
|---|---|---|
| Warm primary | Crimson, solar yellow, amber | Hero, fire character |
| Cool primary | Cobalt, azure, teal | Rival, strategist, water character |
| Monochromatic cool | Silver, slate, black | Assassin, blade master |
| Earth secondary | Forest green, brown, olive | Shinobi, scout |
| Saturated pastel | Pink, magenta, pearl white | Magical/celestial |

## Archetype Matrix

| Archetype | Shape direction | Visual language |
|---|---|---|
| Shonen Martial Hero | Square + triangle | Spiky hair, strong torso, aura |
| Tactical Shinobi | Inverted triangle | Light gear, high collar |
| Wandering Blade Master | Square + sharp | Broad shoulders, sword |
| Shadow Assassin Prodigy | Acute triangles | Sharp hair, daggers |
| Celestial Magical Girl | Curves + stars | Flowing hair, bows, light |
| Tactical Scout Vanguard | Rectangular | Harness, jacket |
| Alchemist Mentor | Rounded/square | Robes, tools, books |
| Cyberpunk Street Samurai | Sharp + rectangular | Tech armor, neon blade |
| Fantasy Archer | Curves + triangles | Cloak, bow |
| Mecha Pilot | Rectangular/mechanical | Structured suit |

## Design Rule

These principles should guide the construction of the data library. Version 1 does not need to calculate shape ratios mathematically.
